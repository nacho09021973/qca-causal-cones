## 0. Answer in one paragraph

Within the declared rows, the substrate fixes a ceiling and the walk decides
where `c` sits below it. Every declared walk on the square, cubic/BCC and
line substrates reaches the ceiling, `c_0 = r_in(C_micro)`. No declared walk
on the hexagonal (triangular-lattice) substrate reaches it, and two walks on
the identical hexagonal substrate (ADMP L6a and JDW L6d: same translation
group, same step set) have different `c_0`, `1/2` and `3/4` in units where
the longest step is 1 — so even at internal dimension `s=2` the substrate
does not determine `c`. In every computed row the strict asymptotic body
equals `C_micro` (all vertex amplitudes survive), so `c_strict^iso = r_in`
and `c_0 <= c_strict^iso`. This is a statement about thirteen declared rows,
not a QCA law (PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS §7).

## 5. Massive rows (Part 3)

Mass parameter `theta` = quasienergy gap at `k=0`, as each source inserts
it: MB coin `e^{-i theta Q}` (L5b); JDW `U_m = U(0,0,-pi/2,phi)
= e^{-i phi sigma_x}` on the left, `theta = 3m/2` (L6c, and L6d "the same
way", JDW p.2), `theta = 3 sqrt3 m/4` (L6f); JDW L6e replaces `U^1` so that
`U(0) = e^{-i m sigma_x}`, `theta = m`.

Method (exact, no matrix-log series). For `U(0) = e^{-i theta Q}`,
`Q^2 = I`, the derivative of the exponential gives
`i dU U(0)^dag = X_par + (sin theta/theta) X_perp`, with `X_par`, `X_perp`
the parts of `dH` commuting and anticommuting with `Q`; hence
`dH = X_par + (theta/sin theta) X_perp`. `c_H(n̂)^2 = n̂.g.n̂` with
`{B_a, B_b} = 2 g_ab I`, `B_a = dH/dk_a(0)` — the linear spatial coefficient
of `H = i log U` at fixed mass, as in the Weyl/Dirac gate. For `omega`,
`G = (U+U^dag)/2` has eigenvalues `cos omega_j`, is scalar at `k=0` and has
zero gradient, so its Hessian gives `omega_j^2 = theta^2 +
(theta/sin theta) q_j |k|^2`. The script certifies
`Hess cos(omega)(0) = -c_0^2 cos(theta) I + sin(theta) K` with `K`
independent of `theta`; an isotropic `c_omega2` exists iff `K = 0`, and then
`c_omega2 = c_0 sqrt(theta cot theta)`.

**Positive control.** On the reviewed minimal 1D Dirac block of the
Weyl/Dirac gate the same code returns `c_H = c_0 M cot M` and `K = 0`
(isotropic `c_omega2`), reproducing `reports/2026-09-14--weyl-dirac-exact-gate.md`
Secs. 5–6.

| Row | linear term at `theta != 0` | `omega^2` curvature at `theta != 0` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L5b (MB, BCC, s=4) | isotropic, `c_H = c_0 theta/sin theta` | `K != 0`: anisotropic and band-split; no isotropic `c_omega2` |
| L6c (JDW) | isotropic, `c_H = c_0 theta/sin theta` | `K = diag(-5 sqrt3/32, 3 sqrt3/32)`: anisotropic |

| control: 1D Dirac | `c_H = c_0 M cot M` | `K = 0`, `c_omega2 = c_0 sqrt(M cot M)` |

Readings, each limited to these rows:

1. Where the mass is a separate coin multiplying a unitary hopping product
   (L5b, L6c, L6d, L6f), `c_H = c_0 theta/sin theta > c_0` for
   `0 < theta < pi`. In the standard Dirac family, where the hopping
   amplitude is scaled by `cos M`, `c_H = c_0 M cot M < c_0`. So `c_H` can
   exceed the ceiling `r_in` in these rows. This contradicts no bound: `c_H`
   is a Hamiltonian coefficient, not a propagation speed.
2. In every massive row computed here, `omega^2` has **no isotropic
   quadratic coefficient** at finite mass; the anisotropy is
   `O(sin theta)` and vanishes as `theta -> 0`, where `c_H`, and the
   isotropic part of the curvature, both return to `c_0`. The standard
   Dirac family is the only computed case with an isotropic `c_omega2`.
   The repo rule "do not treat `c_H` and `c_{omega^2}` as interchangeable"
   is strengthened here: at finite mass there may be no single
   `c_{omega^2}` at all.
3. L6e shows that where the mass is inserted matters: replacing an
   internal coin of the sequence makes the linear term itself anisotropic.

- No statement about doublers or nodes other than `k=0`; no
  finite-momentum dispersion beyond second order; no `V_grp^max`.

  sources' own account (ADMP p.2: spinors on triangle edges). Identifying
  it is left OPEN; the substrate result (triangular group) is a candidate
  explanation, not a proof.

- Convention dependence: the JDW `prod_j` order for Eqs. (2) and (8) is a
  declared reading. The agreement with each source's continuum speed (Sec. 2)
