## 1. Assessment (author's reasoning, 2026-11-12; not established)

**What could have value.**

- Sharpness of the CJWW upper rate for infrared operations: restricting
  operations to the infrared band does not change the exponential rate of
  influence outside the emergent cone. That quantifies how approximate the
  emergent relativistic causality is, which bears on the "relativistic
  low-energy regime" half of the project question.
- The R1 picture: infrared-admissible operations whose leading signal is
  carried by momenta far from zero ("infrared admissible" is not
  "infrared carried"). Conceptually the most interesting point; no
  verified example.
- CJWW state explicitly that they do not prove lower bounds
  (`sources/2408.02108v1.pdf`, PDF p. 3). If nobody else has, the
  operational lower bound is a contribution. `NOVELTY = OPEN`: one targeted
  search only. [UNVERIFIED]

**What holds it back today.**

1. **Admissibility is not proved in the general class.** The headline says
   "infrared-admissible operations"; B1 proves only that the admissibility
   denominators are non-zero (Bravais Stage 1, Sec. 6.3; confirmed by Codex
   twice). This is the most serious gap.
2. **No human verification.** All derivations are Claude's, reviewed by
   models (Grok, Codex). On 2026-11-12 Codex found the H2 misstatement in a
   result Grok had passed.
3. **Fragile statement.** Ten hypotheses and six amendments, most found
   along the way: the natural form of the theorem is probably not found
   yet.
4. **Free walks only.** The project question is about an interacting QCA
   (Thirring, `U = F J`); this line does not reach it.
5. **Diagnostics with defects.** The round-2 rows Z7, Z8, Z10 have stored
   `xi_*`, `v_*`, `K_*` that violate the minimiser equation (gaps 0.23,
   0.11, 0.43; `scripts/README.md`, atlas entry). No conclusion changes
   (those rows fail H3 and H4′ anyway), but the round-2 report is not
   publication material as it stands.

**About 2026-11-12 itself.** The Bravais extension is a routine corollary
with no new idea; its practical effect is to bring W5, Z3 under the theorem
and W2, W3, W6 into the carrier class. The two Codex rounds were worth more
for what they found in B1 (H2) than for Stage 1. A day spent on amendments
and a corollary consolidates but does not move toward the project question
(universal computation and a relativistic regime in the same QCA).
