## 5. What the pass does not establish

The result is weaker than changing local adjacency.

The fourteen one-step CJWW displacements remain the only elementary matter
hops for every geometry state. Therefore:

- the one-step support graph is unchanged;
- the graph distance generated by that hop set is unchanged;
- the strict outer body remains ([-1,1]^3) per step;
- no variable edge length, tetrad, lapse or metric tensor has been derived.

What changes is an **interference-sensitive exact finite-time support**:
different link states can preserve or remove exact cancellations among paths.

This is enough for the gate's declared operational geometricity criterion,
but it is not enough to claim an emergent metric or general relativity.
