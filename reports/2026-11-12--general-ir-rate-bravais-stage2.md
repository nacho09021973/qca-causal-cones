## 2. Which lattice the diagnostics used

All three scripts build the lattice from the walk's steps:
`lattice_data` (`scripts/diagnose_general_ir_rate_polyhedra.py:125-134`)
takes the Hermite normal form of the step exponents and maps it to
physical coordinates, so `basis` spans the lattice generated by the
steps, `L_gen`. Everything that depends on the lattice is computed from
that `basis`:

- the Brillouin-zone grid (`P.bz_grid(basis_np, d)`, round 2 line 82);
- the dual direction test for H3 (`rational_direction`, round 2
  lines 56-58: components of `xi_*` in the dual basis of `L_gen`);
- the plane lattice `L_gen cap u^perp` and the character test (round 2
  lines 143-153);
- `u`, `|u|`, `kappa` in the R1 diagnostic
  (`scripts/diagnose_ir_carrier_r1.py:111-115`, fields `u_int`, `abs_u`).

The column "`Z^d`" of the round-2 table is `isometric_to_Zd(basis)`
(`polyhedra.py:137-159`): whether `L_gen` is congruent to a scaled cubic
lattice. It was the only lattice clause applied, and the label
`OUTSIDE_CLASS_LATTICE` was attached from it (round 2 line 190) after the
hypotheses had been evaluated on `L_gen`.

So the hypothesis columns already are the columns of the Bravais class
with `L = L_gen`. Stage 1 changes only constants (its Sec. 4); the scaling
of `c_r` by `covol(L)^(1/2)` does not affect the non-vanishing conditions
H5, H7, and the band condition U6 removes finitely many `N` and is not a
column. **No row needs a quantity that was not computed.**

## 4. What the rows do not cover

- **Walks defined on a larger lattice.** The rows describe the walk on
  `l^2(L_gen)`. Where the source defines it on a lattice `L` with
  `L_gen` a proper sublattice — carrier Stage A Sec. 9 records this for
  CJWW (5.12) (steps generate the BCC group, per `5213d94`) and for the
  s=4 walk (steps generate the equal-parity lattice of index four) — the
  operator on `l^2(L)` is a direct sum over the cosets of `L_gen`; it is
  also in the Bravais class (with `L`), but its zone is smaller, each
  dominant point of `T_{L_gen}` unfolds into `[L : L_gen]` copies with the
  same eigenvectors, and `u`, `Lambda_0`, characters and offsets are those
  of `L` (Stage 1, Sec. 6, item 2). None of that was computed; the rows
  say nothing about that reading. Which reading is the physical one is a
  question about the model, not about the theorem.
- **Admissibility** of the protocol family in the general class is not
  proved (Stage 1, Sec. 6, item 3); the rows do not touch it.
- Floating point throughout; a dominant point missed by the grid would
  change the H4′ column (round-2 Sec. 4).
