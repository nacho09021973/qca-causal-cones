I need a literature search in mathematical physics. Please give precise bibliographic data
(authors, title, year, arXiv id, journal) and, where you can, the section or page where the
relevant statement appears. Distinguish clearly between what you found in a source and what you
infer. If you are not sure a paper exists, say so; do not guess identifiers.

Context. A research project starts from a specific 3D two-component discrete-time quantum walk
whose low-energy limit near one node is a Weyl fermion. It then tries to couple this matter
sector to an emergent linearized-gravity (helicity ±2) sector, and makes these claims:

1. Emergent helicity ±2 modes with linearized diffeomorphism gauge structure in local qubit/spin
   lattice models already exist (attributed to Z.-C. Gu and X.-G. Wen, arXiv:0907.1203, later
   Nucl. Phys. B 863 (2012) 90–129). Attributed details: an "L-type" model with two physical modes
   and dispersion ω ∝ k³ under analytic control; an "N-type" model argued, with weaker control, to
   give ω ∝ k; a₀₀ and a₀ᵢ introduced as Lagrange multipliers of the scalar and vector
   constraints, with the linearized Einstein action recovered after integrating out the tensor
   momentum; constraints discretized on a cubic lattice and imposed by energy penalties in a finite
   local Hilbert space; and coupling this gravity sector to nearly massless fermions left
   explicitly as future work.
2. Rank-2 symmetric tensor U(1) gauge theories with vector Gauss laws, and the "traceless vector
   charge" theory with two polarizations and cubic dispersion (attributed to M. Pretko, e.g.
   Phys. Rev. B 96, 035119 (2017)).
3. The project-specific claim: coupling an explicit quantum-walk / quantum-cellular-automaton
   Weyl matter sector to such an emergent graviton sector, with the linearized coupling
   (λ/2) h_{μν} T^{μν} and matter stress tensor sourcing the scalar and vector constraints, is not
   done in the literature.

Tasks:

A. For claims 1 and 2, confirm or correct each attributed detail against the papers, with
   section/page. Flag anything that looks misattributed.
B. For claim 3, find prior work that couples quantum walks or QCAs to gravity or to gravitational
   waves, or builds a Weyl/Dirac quantum walk with a dynamical or linearized metric, or defines a
   local stress-energy tensor for a quantum walk or Floquet QCA. Check in particular:
   G. Di Molfetta and F. Debbasch; P. Arnault and F. Debbasch ("Quantum walks and gravitational
   waves", around 2017); P. Arrighi, S. Facchini and M. Forets (quantum walks in curved
   spacetime); G. M. D'Ariano and P. Perinotti and the Pavia QCA programme; T. Farrelly.
C. Other emergent-graviton lattice work: C. Xu (rank-2 "soft graviton" lattice models, around
   2006); C. Xu and P. Hořava (emergent Lifshitz gravity, around 2010); A. Rasmussen, Y.-Z. You and
   C. Xu (around 2016). Say what each establishes about dispersion and control.
D. Known obstructions: how the Weinberg–Witten theorem constrains emergent gravitons coupled to a
   conserved covariant stress tensor, and how Gu–Wen or others evade it. Also the known
   consistency problem of coupling linearized gravity to matter with backreaction (Gupta, Deser).
E. Local energy densities for Floquet systems or quantum walks: is there literature showing that
   a translation-invariant Floquet unitary may admit no local (summable) Hamiltonian logarithm,
   or constructing local conserved densities such as i(U − U†)/2?

Output: one section per task, a final table (paper / what it establishes / relevance to claim
3: anticipates / partially anticipates / unrelated), and a one-paragraph judgement of whether
claim 3 is novel, stating the evidence.
