The claim, as stated, was not found. The closest published s=4 families on the body-centred cubic (BCC) step set are massive Dirac couplings: the continuous parameter is an on-site block, and the massless endpoint is the minimal s=2 walk. No source was found that keeps that step set, drops the on-site term, and lets a massless rank-two cone slope vary below 1/√3. Every item below was opened from arXiv HTML or a publisher abstract, not read as a complete article.

DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.90.062106. Section VII, Eq. (64). Not read in full. Holds fixed the minimal s=2 BCC walk (and its small-mass coupling), not an s>2 massless family.

> “two one-parameter families of automata without completely solving the case.”

Eq. (76) is the block matrix with diagonal blocks s𝒜(k) and off-diagonal blocks i√(1−s²) times the identity. Eq. (77):
> “cos ω± = s(cos kx cos ky cos kz ± sin kx sin ky sin kz).”

Eq. (79), small r=√(1−s²):
> “iγ0 ∂t Ψ = k·γ Ψ + r Ψ,”

and the text calls r the mass. The off-diagonal block is independent of k.
