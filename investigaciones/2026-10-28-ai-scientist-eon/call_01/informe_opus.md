THOUGHT:
The record leaves one precise gap. The Gaussian-tail obstruction (report §3, author-repaired, not re-reviewed) uses only a growth bound on the electric Fourier series. Its own §3.D control, `f=exp[-i theta(z+1/z)]`, `g=1`, shows that the magnetic multiplier alone can be cancelled at finite exponential type. Nobody has located where the obstruction stops, or asked whether the electric chirp D_E closes the gap. The existing proof uses D_E only as a modulus-preserving step.

The one question I propose: in the frozen finite-volume Eon v4 Gauss sector, can a pure state whose electric coefficients have entire Fourier series of finite exponential type be an eigenvector of the exact full step U? This tests a class of references. It does not define an eigenstate, construct a vacuum, use an auxiliary preparation or compute a spectrum. It tells a human which reference-ansatz families are dead before anyone builds them, and where analytic exclusion runs out.

Round-2 attack, applied here:
- **Circularity: passes.** The class is defined by tail bounds, independently of U.
- **Auxiliary preparation: passes.** None is used.
- **Overlap with the Gaussian obstruction: fails as first drafted.** The Round-1 draft headlined extending the exclusion to all zero-type (sub-exponential) tails. That extension is very probably a near-verbatim corollary of the existing Jensen template, with `(log r)²` replaced by `o(r)`. It would mostly re-exclude states the obstruction already covers.
  - **Repair:** zero type and the sub-threshold types are demoted to inherited lemmas to be checked, not results. The live content is restricted to positive finite type at and above the template's threshold, where the phases of D_E matter.

The hardest remaining weakness is not repaired; it is stated as a hard limit. If kicked-rotor-type eigenvectors exist at all, they would plausibly have exponentially decaying tails, outside this class [UNVERIFIED analogy]. A full rejection of the class would therefore prune ansatz families, not decide existence. Ratings below are unverified self-assessments, not evidence or gate verdicts. Nothing has been executed.
