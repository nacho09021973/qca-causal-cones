# W8 / prior-art gauge audit — Gu-Wen and rank-2 tensor gauge theories

**Fecha:** 2026-09-30.  
**Rama:** \`research/gravitational-normalization-gate\`.  
**Estado:** literature/prior-art correction; no novelty claim.

## 1. Gu-Wen overlap

Gu and Wen, *Emergence of helicity +/- 2 modes (gravitons) from qubit models*
(arXiv:0907.1203; Nucl. Phys. B 863 (2012) 90-129) already construct local
qubit/spin models whose low-energy sector has emergent linearized
diffeomorphism gauge structure and gapless helicity +/-2 modes.

Their L-type model has a reliably controlled helicity +/-2 sector with
dispersion proportional to k^3. Their N-type model is argued, with a less
reliable spin-wave/quantum-freeze treatment, to admit a linear omega~k
helicity +/-2 sector.

Therefore the present project must not claim novelty for:

- qubits producing an emergent spin-2/helicity +/-2 sector;
- low-energy linearized diffeomorphism gauge symmetry from a microscopic
  lattice model without that symmetry microscopically;
- the mere existence of a local finite-cutoff graviton-like model.

Gu-Wen explicitly frame the simultaneous emergence of gravity and nearly
massless fermions as a future direction rather than the result established in
that paper.

## 2. Pretko overlap

Pretko, *Generalized Electromagnetism of Subdimensional Particles: A Spin
Liquid Story*, Phys. Rev. B 96, 035119 (2017), reviews four symmetric rank-2
U(1) tensor gauge theories.

The traceless vector-charge theory has

\[
\partial_i E^{ij}=\rho^j,\qquad E^i{}_i=0,
\]

two gapless gauge polarizations, and a cubic dispersion. This is structurally
close to the continuum gauge quotient tested in W8.

Therefore W8's 5 -> 2 mode counting under a traceless vector gauge symmetry
is prior-art structure, not a new graviton construction.

## 3. Methodological correction to W8

The W8 demand for an **exact microscopic diffeomorphism-gauge redundancy** is
stronger than required by an emergence programme.

Gu-Wen provide the relevant precedent: the microscopic qubit model need not
possess the target gauge symmetry exactly; the symmetry may emerge in a
low-energy constrained sector after nonphysical/helicity sectors are gapped
or frozen.

A future gauge gate should therefore require:

1. a controlled low-energy/constraint subspace;
2. an effective Gauss/constraint algebra in that subspace;
3. the target Ward identity to the stated order;
4. stability of the low-energy gauge sector under allowed microscopic
   perturbations.

Exact microscopic gauge symmetry remains sufficient but is no longer treated
as necessary.

## 4. Revised potential contribution of this project

The defensible unresolved target is not “emergent gravitons from qubits.”

It is the bridge

\[
\text{explicit 3D Weyl-limit QCA matter}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{emergent helicity-2 geometry}
\]

with all of the following in one controlled construction:

- exact flat CJWW matter limit;
- geometry-dependent Weyl principal symbol;
- matter-to-geometry backreaction;
- a source derived variationally from the matter/tetrad coupling;
- autonomous geometry propagation;
- an emergent low-energy constraint/gauge sector reducing to the physical
  tensor modes.

Novelty of that combined bridge remains OPEN pending a dedicated literature
audit.

"""Symbolic consistency certificate for theory/W9_CJWW_GRAVITON_MATTER_BRIDGE_GATE.md."""

import sympy as sp


def main():
    lam = sp.symbols("lambda", nonzero=True)
    # Fourier-space momentum k_mu and conserved stress tensor columns.
    w, kx, ky, kz = sp.symbols("omega kx ky kz")
    k = sp.Matrix([w, kx, ky, kz])

    # Treat one fixed nu-column T^{mu nu}; conservation is k_mu T^{mu nu}=0.
    T0, T1, T2, T3 = sp.symbols("T0 T1 T2 T3")
    Tcol = sp.Matrix([T0, T1, T2, T3])
    conservation = sp.expand((k.T*Tcol)[0])

    spatial_div = sp.expand(kx*T1 + ky*T2 + kz*T3)
    assert sp.expand(spatial_div - (conservation - w*T0)) == 0
    print("[PASS] spatial divergence = full conservation residual - omega*T^{0nu}")

    # On the conserved shell, spatial-only coupling leaves -omega*T0 in general.
    spatial_on_shell = sp.expand(spatial_div.subs(T3, (-w*T0-kx*T1-ky*T2)/kz))
    assert sp.simplify(spatial_on_shell + w*T0) == 0
    print("[PASS] spatial-only gauge variation is generically proportional to omega*T^{0nu}")

    # Full linearized metric coupling contracts xi_nu with k_mu T^{mu nu}.
    full_variation = lam * conservation
    full_on_shell = sp.expand(full_variation.subs(T3, (-w*T0-kx*T1-ky*T2)/kz))
    assert sp.simplify(full_on_shell) == 0
    print("[PASS] full h_{mu nu}T^{mu nu} coupling is gauge invariant on conserved stress shell")

    # W6 directional matrix spans all spatial tetrad derivatives.
    D = sp.Matrix([[1,0,0],[1,1,0],[1,1,1]])
    assert D.det() == 1
    print("[PASS] W6 directional currents span all spatial sigma_a p_i tetrad source components")

    print("\nVERDICT: spatial bridge is incomplete; full continuum linearized coupling works once lapse/shift source components are included.")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
