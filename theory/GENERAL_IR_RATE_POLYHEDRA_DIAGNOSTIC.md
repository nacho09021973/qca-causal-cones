manuscript's own regions violate that clause: `B - A = (1,1,1) + B(1/2)`
contains points outside `[-1,1]^3`. The Stage 0 report states that K3 is
inside the class; that statement is wrong as written. No step of Stages 1

the correction of the parent charter (dropping the clause or replacing it

## Region rule (fixed before computing)

`V` = a vertex of `conv F` (the first in the script's order that lies on a
symmetry axis of the walk), `n = V/|V|`. Alice: ball at the origin, radius
`rho = |V| / (4 sqrt3)` (the manuscript's ratio). Bob: ball at `V`, same
radius. Then `v_* = V (1 - 1/(2 sqrt3))` if the minimiser lies on the axis.
Resource internal vector: each of the two basis vectors of the nodal space
at `k = 0` (reported separately). No change of regions after seeing the
output.

## Quantities and labels

Per walk: node and cone (isotropy, untilted); `Phi(t n)` by grid plus local
refinement over the Brillouin zone of `L`; `t_*` from `d/dt Phi(t n) = |v_*|`;
`K_*`; dominant points at `xi_*` (count, spectral gap, Hessian, whether
`k = 0` is one, character on the plane lattice `L cap n^perp`); gradients
of `log|lambda|` at each dominant point against `v_*` (H8); left-vector

from `rho |u|` with `u` the primitive dual vector along `n`; the
off-axis check of `grad Phi(xi_*)` (H1 by symmetry).
