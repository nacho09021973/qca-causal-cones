# Amendment 3 to the general infrared operational rate charter — DRAFT

2026-11-12. Drafted by Claude (claude-opus-5-5). **Status: DRAFT; not in
force until approved by the user.** The frozen charter (`aa6c5f6`) and
Amendments 1–2 are not edited.

## Reason

The leading term at zero momentum carries a factor `e^(i N psi)` and a
complex depth factor `Gamma_0`
(`reports/2026-11-12--general-ir-rate-stage2-leading-term.md`, Secs. 5,
7). If every admissible choice of `(M, M_B)` gives coefficients on one
real line, the measured imaginary part can tend to zero for all `N`. The
condition `psi in pi Z` suggested in the progress record is not sufficient
when `Gamma_0` is complex, and is not needed when the coefficient can be
rotated. Written after the leading-term derivation, and declared so.

## Change

- **H10 (rotation).** Among the protocols in the real span of the declared
  family for which the leading coefficients at the trivial dominant points
  other than `eta = 0` vanish, the coefficient `c` at `eta = 0` takes two
  values that are not real multiples of each other.

  Sufficient: H6 holds for the left dominant eigenvectors, and the right
  dominant eigenvector `e_0` is not a complex multiple of
  `R~ = R_0(w_B)`.

No condition on `psi` is added. K3 satisfies H10 (same report, Sec. 8).
Nothing else changes.
