## 1. Single question

Can the **currently frozen inhomogeneous/QCA construction** produce, from its
own microscopic degrees of freedom, enough gravitational normalization to
break the flat scale degeneracy?

Concretely, can it derive both:

1. a dynamical metric/gravitational response coefficient rather than a
   prescribed background profile; and
2. an independent dimensionless curvature/length anchor,

so that the common rescaling freedom in `a` and `tau` is removed without
setting a Planck scale, Newton constant, AdS radius, or the observed value of
`c` by fiat?

## 3. G0 — dynamical-geometry prerequisite

Before any Einstein-Hilbert or AdS normalization calculation, determine
whether the frozen model contains a geometric variable that is itself
dynamical.

PASS requires all three:

1. a microscopic geometric degree of freedom `g` (or equivalent) belongs to
   the state/update algebra, rather than being an externally specified field;
2. the update rule evolves `g`;
3. matter/information changes the evolution of `g` (backreaction).

A prescribed `vartheta(X)`, position-dependent coin, lapse, tetrad, metric,
or AdS profile does **not** pass G0 merely because matter propagates in it.

**Stop rule:** if G0 fails, terminate as
`BLOCKED_NO_DYNAMICAL_GEOMETRY`. Do not write an Einstein-Hilbert
coefficient for the frozen model.

## 4. G1 — gravitational normalization

Only if G0 passes, derive a long-wavelength action or equivalent response law
with a coefficient fixed by the microscopic update.

For a 3+1 dimensional continuum convention
[
\frac{S_{\rm EH}}{\hbar}
=
\frac{c^3}{16\pi\hbar G}
\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\,R,
]
a lattice derivation may be summarized by a dimensionless coefficient
`Gamma` only after the discretization convention is stated.

The gate must distinguish:

- `Gamma` derived from a microscopic response function;
- `Gamma` assumed by EFT matching;
- a statement that Einstein-Hilbert is the allowed leading operator, which
  does **not** by itself determine `Gamma`.

## 5. G2 — independent curvature/length anchor

A gravitational normalization alone is insufficient to determine a velocity
scale from `hbar` and `G`.

Require a second independently derived dimensionless quantity, e.g.
[
\lambda_\Lambda = |\Lambda|a^2
]
or an equivalent `L/a`, generated by the same microscopic theory rather
than imposed as a background profile.

In 3+1 dimensions, if a consistent discretization gives
[
\Gamma =
\frac{c^3 a^2}{16\pi\hbar G},
\qquad
\lambda_\Lambda=|\Lambda|a^2,
]
then elimination of `a` would give the conditional anchor relation
[
c^3 =
16\pi\,\Gamma\,
\frac{\hbar G|\Lambda|}{\lambda_\Lambda}.
]

This equation is only a **criterion**. It is not a result of the current QCA
unless both `Gamma` and `lambda_\Lambda` are microscopically derived.

## 6. G3 — non-circular physical identification

Even if G1 and G2 pass, the bridge to nature must not insert the desired
answer through the dictionary.

Forbidden as derivations:

- `a = l_P` by declaration;
- `tau = t_P` by declaration;
- choosing `a/tau = c`;
- choosing an AdS radius in SI without an independently justified map;
- importing a measured lattice spacing from a different QCA model.

The physically meaningful claim is a dimensionless relation among independently
defined observables/constants, not the decimal representation
`299792458 m/s` itself.

## 8. Claim ceiling

prove AdS/CFT, reopen H1/E, or predict the SI numeral assigned to `c`.

Its only purpose is to decide whether the current model contains a
non-circular route from dimensionless QCA dynamics to an independently
anchored gravitational scale.
