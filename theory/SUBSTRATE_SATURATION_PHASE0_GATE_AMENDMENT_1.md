## 0. Pre-step check

- **Known.** Frozen Sec. 2.2 defines «admissible» as membership in a
  declared class of the cited frozen contracts plus an isotropic untilted
  node in the sense of Sec. 2.4. Frozen Sec. 2.4 takes `c_0` «as certified
  per row in the c-across-lattices report Sec. 4», and Sec. 5 requires the
  fields to be filled «from promoted exact data». L4 is re-tabulated, not
  computed, in that report (l. 147), so no promoted certificate covers its
  node. DeepSeek (addendum review, «advertencia formal») noted that the
  clauses (i)–(v) do not by themselves establish membership of L4 in a
  declared class.
- **Question.** Under what explicit conditions may a declared row whose
  node is not covered by a promoted certificate be classified as
  admissible, and does L4 meet them?
- **What would sink this amendment for L4.** No frozen clause can be cited
  that declares the L4 walks as a class; or a derivation step fails on
  review; or the substrate of L4 cannot be stated under frozen Sec. 2.3. In
  any of these cases L4 stays unclassified and that is recorded (user rule
  of 2026-11-03: do not force L4 to obtain `DEFINITIONS_CLOSED`).

## 1. Rule (replaces nothing; adds a second admissible source for the node)

A declared `s = 2`, `d <= 3` row whose isotropic untilted node is **not**
covered by a promoted certificate may be classified as admissible in the
sense of frozen Sec. 2.2 **only if all** of the following hold, each cited:

1. **Class.** The row identifies the pertinent declared class by quoting
   the frozen clause that declares it (contract, commit, `file:line`)
   together with that contract's admission rule for the class. The
   appearance of the row in a table, a report or `CURRENT_STATE.md`, by
   itself, does **not** suffice.
2. **Primary source.** Each node clause (i)–(v) of frozen Sec. 2.4 is
   derived from the symbol as printed in the primary source (`file.pdf
   p.N`, equation number), with the momentum convention stated; the
   transcription of the equations used has been checked literally against
   the PDF.
3. **Review.** The derivation and the transcription have an independent
   review by a model of a different family from the author's, or by a
   person, recorded with the SHA-256 of the reviewed text and the base
   commit, with verdict PASS and no open mandatory repair.
4. **Substrate.** The substrate of each walk is stated under frozen
   Sec. 2.3 (translation group generated by one complete update, with its
   composite step set), read from the printed symbol; a disagreement with
   the source's lattice label is recorded, not repaired.
5. **Pair unchanged.** The pair `(r_in, c_0)` is still taken from the
   single source fixed by frozen Sec. 2.4; the derivation may show
   consistency with it but is not a second source for it.

A row classified under this rule is marked in the audit as «admissible
under Amendment 1 (reviewed primary-source derivation)», never as
«certified». The rule does not apply to rows with `s != 2`, `d > 3`, or
without a frozen class clause, and it gives no row any confirmatory weight
for (PH).

## 2. Application to L4 (as drafted; to be checked on review)

1. **Class.** `theory/C_ACROSS_LATTICES_GATE.md` (preregistration commit
   `30672ba`), Sec. 2 table, l. 78: row L4, `d = 3`, «Z^3,
   `C_micro=[-1,1]^3`», «CJWW `2408.02108v1` (5.12), (5.15)», `s = 2`. Its
   admission rule, l. 100–104: «Only walks whose symbols are read from a
   primary source equation are admitted. No walk is constructed for this
   gate». The declared class of L4 is therefore the two printed walks
   CJWW (5.12) and (5.15), with no parameters. Frozen Sec. 2.2 of phase 0
   names C_ACROSS_LATTICES_GATE among the contracts whose declared classes
   form the domain.
2. **Primary source.** Addendum Sec. 1–2: (5.12) on PDF p. 16, (5.15) on
   PDF p. 17; clauses (i)–(v) derived by hand for both walks, convention
   `T_a := i ∂_{p_a} W(0)` (sign immaterial to every clause). Literal check
   of (5.12) and (5.14) on p. 16 by DeepSeek, PASS (`801c3f2`), including
   the confirmation that the `p_1` typo of (5.14) is in the original.
3. **Review.** DeepSeek (`deepseek-v4-pro`), two PASS verdicts, no
   mandatory repairs (`debates/2026-11-03--substrate-saturation-phase0-addendum-L4-review-deepseek/`,
   `debates/2026-11-03--substrate-saturation-phase0-addendum-L4-p16-check-deepseek/`).
   DeepSeek is a different family from the author (Claude). One house.
4. **Substrate: author finding, hand derivation, `[UNVERIFIED]` until
   reviewed.** Read from the printed symbols:
   - (5.12) is the product of `cos p_a + i sin p_a σ_a`, each factor with
     Fourier support `{±e_a}`; the composite support is exactly
     `{±1}^3`. The group it generates is `{x ∈ Z^3 : x_1 ≡ x_2 ≡ x_3
     (mod 2)}`, the BCC lattice, which up to the scale `1/sqrt3` is the
     substrate of L3 and L5a (`N_3`, CS l. 486). Its scalar part
     `cos p_1 cos p_2 cos p_3 + sin p_1 sin p_2 sin p_3`
     (`verify_directional_saturation.py` l. 81, `F1`) has the form of the
     d = 3 published symbol with chirality `+1`
     (`verify_weyl_dirac_gate.py` l. 68); whether (5.12) coincides with
     one of the L3 symbols up to the declared symmetries is not checked
     here.
   - (5.15) has entries `cos p_1 cos p_2 e^{±i p_3}` (steps `(±1,±1,±1)`),
     `cos p_1 sin p_2` (steps `(±1,±1,0)`) and `sin p_1` (steps
     `(±1,0,0)`). These generate `Z^3`. The convex hull is still
     `[-1,1]^3`.
   - Hence the source label «Z^3» of C_ACROSS l. 78 holds for (5.15) and
     not for (5.12). Both walks have `C_micro` a cube, so the shape column
     of the audit is unchanged; the substrate count of audit F3 changes:
     the «cube substrate of L4» is the `Z^3` group of (5.15), and (5.12)
     is a further walk on the BCC substrate.
5. **Pair.** `(r_in, c_0) = (1/sqrt3, 1/sqrt3)` from the c-across-lattices
   table l. 147, as before; the addendum's CJWW-unit pair `(1, 1)` is
   shown only for consistency.
