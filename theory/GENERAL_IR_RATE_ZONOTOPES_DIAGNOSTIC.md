## Construction (new, declared before computing)

For directions `a_1, ..., a_n` (physical vectors) and unit Bloch vectors
`n_j = a_j / |a_j|` (embedded in `R^3` when `d = 2`), the walk is the
ordered product

    S(k) = prod_{j=1..n} exp(i (k.a_j) n_j.sigma),   s = 2.

Each factor is `(1/2)(I + n_j.sigma) e^(i k.a_j) + (1/2)(I - n_j.sigma) e^(-i k.a_j)`,
unitary and finite range, so `F` is contained in the sums `sum ±a_j` and
`conv F` is the zonotope of the `a_j` (exact support checked by the
script). `S(0) = I`; the linear term is `-sum_j (k.a_j) n_j.sigma`, an
untilted cone, isotropic iff `sum_j a_j a_j^T / |a_j|` is a multiple of
the identity (a weighted tight frame). Only such sets are used. This is a
standard construction (CJWW Eq. (5.12) is the case of the three axes); no
novelty is claimed.

| Z1 | 2 | (1,0), (0,1), (1,1), (1,-1) | octagon (edges 1, sqrt2) |

Note on Z5: to keep ten distinct bodies, Z5 is the octagon of Z1 with the
four factors in the order (1,1), (1,0), (1,-1), (0,1); same zonotope,
different walk. It tests whether the factor order, not the body, decides
the outcome.

## Region rule and labels

As in the parent charter, with one declared fallback: if no maximal step
passes the rate-symmetry test, the first maximal step is used and the row
is labelled `NO_RATE_AXIS`; the on-axis condition is then only the
numerical check `H1_on_axis`. Same hypotheses, tolerances and labels.
Lattice: generated by the steps; rows whose lattice is not isometric to
`Z^d` (up to scale) are outside the declared class and flagged.
