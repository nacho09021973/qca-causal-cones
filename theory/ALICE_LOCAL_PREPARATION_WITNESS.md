\[

## The causal origin is the encoding event

This is the load-bearing definitional step and the place an adversarial
reviewer must attack. It is therefore stated as an explicit obligation.

**Exogeneity.** The binary input is supplied at Alice at `T = 2`,
independently of the prepared state. No initial state, preparation map,
timing or earlier operation is conditioned on it. This is an operational
stipulation about the protocol, not a claim about metaphysical free choice.

**Identical pre-choice histories.** With
`rho_initial = |0,down><0,down|`, both conditional histories satisfy, as

\[
\rho^{(0)}(T)=\rho^{(1)}(T)=S^T\rho_{\rm initial}(S^{\dagger})^{T},
\qquad T=0,1,2^- .
\tag{2}
\]

Consequently **every** observable of the model has identical statistics
before the encoding event, and the mutual information between the bit and
any pre-choice observable is exactly zero. There is no earlier encoding of
the bit in the matter state. The conditional histories first differ at
`T = 2^+`, where `rho^(b)(2^+) = U_b |chi><chi| U_b^dagger` with

conserving. All later dynamics are again common to both branches.

**What this licenses, and what it does not.** Because the shared,
