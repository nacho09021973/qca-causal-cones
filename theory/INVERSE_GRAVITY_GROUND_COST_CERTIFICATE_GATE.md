local ground-state inequalities and stationarity, without separately knowing
E0. Establish the certificate's sufficiency and test whether using only
individual unitary Weyl variations can certify a strictly positive lower cost.

An actual N-type bound requires explicit coefficients and a verified operator
identity. The existence of a certificate framework is not such a bound.
No finite-support optimization or auxiliary script is authorized by this
analytic contract; numerical search would require a frozen operator list,
couplings and exact reconstruction/error contract first.

a stronger sufficient route, not a necessary condition for a bound at p_L alone.
State this distinction explicitly. A positive uniform lower cost would refute
the quartic numerator route, not all soft excitations or the model.
Stop with OPEN if no actual N-type positive identity is obtained. Do not replace
the missing proof by another symbol calculation or an unbounded SDP proposal.
Evidence is author-side; no independent review or push is implied.
